[DEMO RESULTS NOTICE]
This model was trained on a synthetic demonstration dataset (sample_dataset.csv).
Synthetic samples intentionally showcase characteristic phishing patterns
(raw IPs, credentials @, multi-level subdomains, keywords), which makes the
classes distinctly separable and yields high demo metrics (Accuracy/F1 ~ 99%+).

REAL DATASET COMPARISON NOTE (VIVA DEFENSE):
In real-world deployment on millions of unconstrained URLs (e.g. PhishTank + Tranco),
metrics will naturally exhibit lower recall (typically 88% - 94%) because modern
attackers utilize compromised benign domains, dynamic shorteners, and newly registered
domains with minimal lexical cues. To train on live real-world data, run:
  python build_real_dataset.py --download
  python train_model.py --dataset real_dataset.csv
