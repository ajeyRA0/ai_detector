Academic & Viva Defense Notes

Why Lexical URL Features Only? Phishing campaigns frequently deploy zero-day server setups that evade network crawlers by checking visitor IP headers or blocking automated bots (bot cloaking). Lexical and structural inspection is instantaneous (sub-millisecond), operates completely air-gapped without leaking user browsing habits to external networks, and cannot be tricked by dynamic page redirects.
